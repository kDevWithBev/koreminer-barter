KOREMINER

The Shaft Barter Card Lore

The cut

Other chains asked a store of value to wait. Bitcoin showed that twenty-one million could be enough, if nobody could strike coin twenty-one million and one. Keeta cut a different tunnel. A token here is native. Transfers settle at the speed of Keeta, and one block can carry a whole crew of them. The picks are not a picture of work. They are the work.

What KORE is

KORE is the ore. Twenty-one million pieces, native on Keeta, cut to the same idea as Bitcoin’s store of value: a hard cap, a halving clock, and no committee that can add more. It is not wrapped Bitcoin. It can move as fast as Keeta moves.

The seal

The whole supply was struck once. Then the mint was sealed. The only key that could make more KORE was thrown down a hole with no bottom. Twenty-one million. Then none.

What the miner does

A pick is a real transfer on Keeta mainnet. The shaft packs many picks into one block. Miners bring KTA. The shaft spends it as picks. KORE is the pay, when the vein opens.

The vein

The supply is mined across four years, from 10 October 2026 until the vein is empty in 2030. Each year the cut is half of the year before. Same shape as Bitcoin’s halvings. A shorter tunnel.

Each day the shaft cuts a slice of that year’s supply and splits it across the miners who sent fuel. When the fourth year ends, the picks can continue, but no new KORE is struck. The coins already paid can still move.

How you mine

1. Use a Keeta wallet you control. Looking up an address is free and starts nothing.
2. Send KTA to the shaft. KTA is the fuel. It is spent on picks. It does not come back.
3. The shaft turns that fuel into packed transfers on mainnet. More fuel, more of the shift.
4. KORE is paid to the same address that sent the KTA. The day’s ore is split by fuel, not by titles.

KORE is paid in whole coins. A send costs the same fuel for a fraction as for a pile, so a wallet under one coin is not paid. The fraction stays on that wallet and is paid when it makes the next coin. It is not given to anyone else. The lookup shows how far that wallet is from the next coin. It cannot name a KTA deposit, because the split changes when other fuel arrives or burns.

Copying an address lets you look at the board. It cannot spend that wallet’s KTA, and it cannot take that wallet’s KORE.
