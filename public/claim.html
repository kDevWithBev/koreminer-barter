Claim a Base deposit

Use this page only if you sent KTA from Base, through the bridge, and the destination was the shaft. The bridge signed that deposit. KORE cannot cross back to Base, and it will not be paid to the bridge.

You need a Keeta wallet first. Create a keeta_ address you control, then come back. Connect the same Base wallet that bridged the KTA and sign a message. No gas. No token moves on Base. Payment is not instant. It can take an additional 24 hours.

Only the Base address that actually bridged the fuel is accepted. Any other Base address is refused. Do not paste a seed or a private key on this page.

The amount waiting is shown here when the shaft has a Base deposit on hold.

Check the Keeta address. This cannot be undone.

The first signature wins. If that keeta_ address is wrong, the KORE is still paid there. Payment is not instant. It can take an additional 24 hours.
